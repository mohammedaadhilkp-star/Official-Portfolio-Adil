A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

What kind of projects can we work on?+

I work on Meta and Google Ads, SEO, social media, motion graphics, video, and website design and development. Share your goals and we can work out the right scope.

Do you work with clients outside Kerala?+

Yes. We can collaborate remotely through online calls, email, and WhatsApp. Let me know your location and time zone when you get in touch.

What should I include in my enquiry?+

Tell me about your business, the service you need, your main goals, and any deadline or budget you have in mind. It’s fine if you’re still figuring things out.

How much will my project cost?+

Pricing depends on the scope, deliverables, and timeline. After understanding your requirements, I’ll share a tailored proposal for you to review.

How does consultation booking work?+

Choose a preferred date and time in IST, then send the request on WhatsApp. I’ll confirm availability and share the call details. The request does not automatically reserve a slot.

How soon will I hear back?+

I usually reply within 24 hours. If your project is time-sensitive, mention your deadline in the message.

Are you open to full-time opportunities?+

Yes. I’m open to full-time roles, including Saudi Arabia and remote opportunities, as well as freelance projects. Share the role and relevant details by email.

What is performance marketing? +

Performance marketing focuses on measurable outcomes such as leads, enquiries and sales. I plan, track and optimise paid campaigns around your business goals, combining audience targeting, creative testing and ongoing campaign improvements.

How long until I see results? +

Timelines depend on the channel, your starting point and your goals. Paid campaigns can begin generating data and enquiries after launch, while SEO and organic content usually need sustained work. We’ll agree on realistic milestones for your project.

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)? +

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) helps make your content clear and useful for people asking specific questions. I structure website content and FAQs so search engines and answer tools can understand your services and surface relevant information.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)? +

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) helps make your brand information understandable to AI-powered search platforms. My approach combines clear content, structured information and consistent business details to support discoverability.

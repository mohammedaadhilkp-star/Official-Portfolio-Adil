What is performance marketing? +

Performance marketing is advertising where you pay for measurable actions — leads, purchases, downloads or calls — rather than passive views. Every rupee is tracked and optimised for clear ROI.

How much does digital marketing cost in Kerala? +

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)? +

AEO optimizes content so AI search engines and answer assistants directly select your business as the authoritative answer for user questions.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)? +

GEO formats your brand, schema and web citations so LLMs like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity cite and recommend your business to prospective buyers.
